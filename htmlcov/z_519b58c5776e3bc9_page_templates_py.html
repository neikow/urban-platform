1"""Ready-made "À propos" pages (see ``core.page_templates``). 

2 

3Built from the home page parts. Where only the association knows the facts, 

4the text says what to write: editors replace these instructions with their 

5text, and delete the parts they do not need. The members of the commission 

6and of the development team are shown after the content, so their templates 

7end on a part that introduces them. 

8""" 

9

17def text(html: str) -> RawBlock: 

18 return {"type": "text", "value": html} 

19 

20 

21def paragraph_section(label: str, title: str, html: str, **kwargs: str) -> RawBlock: 

22 return section(label=label, title=title, content=[{"type": "text", "value": html}], **kwargs)
